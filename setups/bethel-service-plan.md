# Bethel service plan for the auto director

How each part of a Bethel church service is shot automatically. Decided with the
production lead, November 2026. This is the brief the auto director is built to.

## Service order and shooting style

| Part | What happens | How the cameras behave |
|---|---|---|
| Opening songs (1-2) | Worship leader leads | Creative mode: leader shots, slow moves on piano keys and guitar frets (brief), fades or cuts by the song's feel |
| Prayer | Deacon prays at the platform | **Cam 3 on the deacon** for the whole prayer; slow fade in and out; no motion |
| Offering | Deacon walks among the people; leader keeps singing | **Stay on the platform**: leader, band, instruments; gentler pace than the opening songs; no congregation shots |
| Congregational song | Leader | Creative mode |
| The special | Soloist sings | Soloist-focused: tight shots, slow push-ins, longest shots, slowest fades |
| Transition song | Minister comes up | Follow the minister to the pulpit, then settle |
| Scripture reading | Minister reads, seats the people | Steady medium shot, little or no motion |
| Preaching (about an hour) | Minister | Sermon mode: smooth tracking if he walks; calm cuts between a medium and a wider shot every minute or two; no creative moves |
| Closing songs (1-2) | Worship team returns | Creative mode, gentler |
| Altar prayer (sometimes) | People come forward during closing worship | **Stay wide**: one wide shot of the stage and altar, slow fades, no close-ups of people |
| Dismissal | | Wide |

## Knowing which part it is

- From the Dante mic channels (the DL32R routes 1 to 1 to Dante): pulpit mic with no music is
  preaching; the deacon's mic with no music is prayer; music with the special singer's mic is
  the special; music with the worship leader's mic is worship.
- Backup: a row of part buttons (Worship, Prayer, Special, Sermon, Altar) anyone in the booth
  can tap to correct it.
- Channel list: to come (screenshots of the DL32R channel names).

## Song feel

No click track and no Planning Center, so the feel (slow, medium, fast) comes from listening
to the worship mix on Dante: beat estimate over the first 10-20 seconds, kept up to date as the
song builds. Medium until sure.

## Cameras

- Cams 1, 2, 4: RS4 Pro gimbals on Middle Control, fully automatic.
- Cam 3: Studio 4K + Xeen on a tripod, not motorised. Framed and focused before service on
  the pulpit, where the deacon always prays. The same shot serves the prayer, the scripture
  reading and the steady sermon shot.

## Open questions

- Clips of a slow song, a fast song, the special and a few minutes of preaching, to measure
  shot lengths, fade lengths and framing.
